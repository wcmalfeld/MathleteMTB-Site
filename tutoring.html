My Approach

Every student learns differently. I focus on building strong fundamentals, explaining the “why” behind the math, and helping students feel genuinely capable. While succeeding on tests is important, understanding the problem solving process and figuring out how one learns best is what I focus on.

Sessions are relaxed but focused. We move at the pace that actually works for you or your student, revisiting the basics when needed and pushing ahead when things click.

Why Mountain Biking + Math

Mountain biking and math have more in common than people expect. Both reward patience, both involve reading the terrain ahead of you, and both get easier — and more fun — the more comfortable you get with falling down and trying again.

Learning math is a lot like learning to ride. You don’t become confident by only doing things you already know how to do. You become confident by trying something difficult, making mistakes, figuring out what went wrong, and trying again.

My job isn’t to make sure a student never gets a problem wrong. It’s to help them become comfortable enough with being wrong that they’re willing to keep trying.

Questions

What happens in a session?

We meet on Zoom and work on a shared digital whiteboard, so we can both write, draw diagrams and work through problems step by step. Bring homework, an upcoming test, or a topic you want to understand better.

What does my student need?

A computer or tablet with a reliable internet connection and Zoom. Paper and a pencil are always handy. The Zoom link comes in the booking confirmation email.

Who should make the booking?

If the student is under 18, please have a parent or guardian book the session and use their own email address. Confirmations and session details go to that address.

How does payment work?

You pay when you book, through Stripe. You’ll see the price before you pay, and card details go straight to Stripe; Mathlete MTB never sees or stores them.

What if we need to reschedule?

Your confirmation email has links to reschedule or cancel. If anything else comes up, email wcmalfeld@gmail.com.

Not sure which subject to pick?

Pick the closest one and describe what you need when you book, or send me a note first and we’ll figure out the right starting point together.

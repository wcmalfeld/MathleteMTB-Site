Your information

Bookings are handled by Calendly and payments by Stripe. Card details go straight to Stripe; Mathlete MTB never sees or stores them. The details you enter are used only to schedule and run your sessions and to contact you about them, and are never sold or shared for marketing.

If the student is under 18, a parent or guardian should make the booking with their own email address. Please share only what’s needed to prepare for the session: no grades, health information or other sensitive details.

  • Mathlete Ski

Students change a ski's sidecut radius and edge angle, the skier's speed and mass, then press Start turn to watch the skier carve it. The turn radius, centripetal acceleration, lateral g and required inward force all come from three equations, R = Rsc cos θ, a = v²/R and F = ma, worked through live with the student's numbers.

  • Simulation first. Tip the ski and the line tightens; double the speed and the acceleration arrow grows four times. Linked turns, pause-anywhere vectors, and a true-scale view.
  • A Graph Lab alongside. Radius vs. edge angle, acceleration and force vs. speed, with the current setup marked, plus a side-by-side comparison of two setups.
  • Five challenges. Each starts with a prediction, then an experiment, the maths and a written explanation. Points reward the reasoning as well as the right answer.
  • Honest about the model. It's an idealized carve, and the lab says what it leaves out (flex, snow, skidding). It's for learning the maths, not for ski advice.
